Feasibility & safeguards

These are implementation principles from the pitch deck.

FEASIBILITY

Integration-first

Preserve existing government scholarship portals and connect each source independently through adapters.

RULES

Configurable eligibility

Keep scheme rules configurable rather than hard-coded, so policy changes can be handled more safely.

TRUST

API-grounded status

Prevent the AI from inventing application or payment status; show source-backed information.

HUMAN-IN-LOOP

Review uncertain matches

Low-confidence entity matching can enter a human review queue.

RISK

Restricted production access

Use authorized APIs, sandbox environments or clearly labelled mock adapters during demonstrations.

RISK

Privacy & security

Use minimum-necessary data, RBAC, encryption, secure document handling, audit trails and rate limiting.
